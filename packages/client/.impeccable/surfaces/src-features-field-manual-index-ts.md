# Field Manual

Read surface extending the existing native Babylon gallery. Players look up
the current Eastern Collective roster and shared Resource Extractor without
leaving or discarding a paused match. No identity redesign or new raster assets.

Search and category remain above an original-icon roster, with selected compiled
statistics and weapon details alongside it on desktop. The list occupies roughly
a third of the gallery; narrow layouts stack. Selecting an entry moves reading
focus to its heading. Back to search restores search focus. Contextual links
align the requested entry at the reading viewport's top. Exact modifiers
stay in expandable details, with target and final-damage qualifications.

Inspect model opens the actual installed GLB in an isolated orbit/zoom scene,
with native controls beside it or above it when narrow. Escape closes only that
view and restores its initiating control. Fetch failure has Retry and Back;
late loading is cancelled/disposed. No simulation, content values or visibility
rules change. Prices and relationships have one source: compiled content.

The route is /manual, also available from Home and the paused match menu.
The current gallery includes 15 actor references and four concept/control pages.
Contextual hints and selection links reuse this controller and preserve its
paused match. Playtest and training integrations remain separate work.
