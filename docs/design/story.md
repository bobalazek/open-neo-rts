# Story

## Background

In 2032 a global economic collapse broke the world order. Three power blocs rose from it, each fighting for dwindling resources and for quantum computing technology that could reshape warfare.

- **Alliance of Free Nations:** Western democracies that kept their technological edge: drones, cyber warfare, precision strikes, but limited manpower and resources.
- **Eastern Collective:** a merger of Asian industrial powerhouses: overwhelming force through mass production, adaptable hybrid warfare and electromagnetic weapons.
- **Phoenix Initiative:** private military companies, tech corporations and resistance movements from failed states: guerrilla tactics, salvage, mercenaries and experimental weapons.

How each faction plays is in [factions/](factions/README.md).

## The war

| Phase                  | Years     | What happens                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| ---------------------- | --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The Silicon War**    | 2035–2036 | The Phoenix Initiative reveals a quantum computing breakthrough built from salvaged tech in the Silicon Valley wasteland. It threatens to make all military encryption useless, so the Alliance and the Collective both move to seize or destroy it |
| **Operation Blackout** | 2036–2037 | The Collective fires electromagnetic pulse weapons across major cities, crippling the Alliance's systems. The Alliance answers with the EMP-immune Ghost Drone program                                                                              |
| **The Adaptive War**   | 2037–2038 | The Phoenix Initiative's nanite technology rebuilds and modifies military hardware from battlefield debris, and every faction races to adapt                                                                                                        |

## Campaigns

| Campaign           | Faction                  | Lead                | Theme                                                                         |

The campaigns interweave: choices in one affect the others, which rewards playing all three. Campaigns come after skirmish and multiplayer ([roadmap.md](../project/roadmap.md)).

## How the story shows up in play

- **Victory conditions:** Territorial Control is about holding quantum computing facilities, the prize the whole war is fought over ([objectives-and-victory.md](objectives-and-victory.md)).
- **Superweapons:** the Collective's EMP Blackout Array is Operation Blackout on the battlefield; the Alliance's Ghost Drones resist EMP after research.
- **Weather and terrain:** dynamic weather and destructible environments are part of the battlefield ([terrain-and-biomes.md](terrain-and-biomes.md)).
