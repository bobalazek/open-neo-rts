# World of 2035 (source document)

> The original world-building summary for the base game in the `core` pack: lore, factions, campaigns and the signature roster. It's the **source of truth for names and story**. The faction docs ([afn.md](afn.md), [ec.md](ec.md), [pi.md](pi.md)) turn it into playable rules; where they disagree on names or flavour, this document wins, and the faction docs are updated to match.

## World Background - 2035

In the wake of a devastating global economic collapse in 2032, the world order has fractured into three distinct power blocs, each vying for control of dwindling resources and revolutionary quantum computing technology that could reshape the future of warfare.

## Factions

### The Alliance of Free Nations (AFN)

A coalition of Western democracies that maintained their technological edge through the crisis. They possess superior drone technology and cyber warfare capabilities, but struggle with limited manpower and resources.

**Strengths:**

- Advanced AI-driven combat systems
- Stealth technology
- Precision strikes
- Superior air power

**Signature Units:**

- Quantum Network Command Center
- Ghost Drone Swarms
- Cyber Warfare Specialists
- Hypersonic Strike Jets

### The Eastern Collective (EC)

Formed from the merger of several Asian industrial powerhouses, the EC emphasizes overwhelming force through mass production and adaptable hybrid warfare.

**Strengths:**

- Rapid deployment capabilities
- Versatile unit modifications
- Superior numbers
- Advanced electromagnetic weapons

**Signature Units:**

- Mobile Factory Complexes
- Metamorphic Battle Tanks
- EMP Artillery
- Drone-Hunter Specialists

### The Phoenix Initiative (PI)

A decentralized network of private military companies, tech corporations, and resistance movements that arose from the ashes of failed states. They specialize in unconventional warfare and adaptive technology.

**Strengths:**

- Guerrilla tactics
- Salvage and upgrade systems
- Mercenary recruitment
- Experimental weapons

**Signature Units:**

- Nanite Reconstruction Vehicles
- Insurgent Battalions
- Black Market Superweapons
- Adaptive Combat Systems

## Core Narrative

### Phase 1: The Silicon War (2035-2036)

The conflict ignites when the Phoenix Initiative reveals their breakthrough in quantum computing, developed from salvaged tech in the Silicon Valley wasteland. This advancement threatens to render traditional military encryption useless, prompting both the AFN and EC to launch simultaneous operations to secure or destroy this technology.

### Phase 2: Operation Blackout (2036-2037)

The war escalates when the EC deploys their electromagnetic pulse weapons across major cities, temporarily crippling AFN's advanced systems. This forces the AFN to adapt their strategies and leads to the development of their Ghost Drone program, immune to conventional EMP attacks.

### Phase 3: The Adaptive War (2037-2038)

The Phoenix Initiative introduces their nanite technology, allowing them to rapidly rebuild and modify military assets from battlefield debris. This development transforms the nature of warfare, as all factions race to adapt their strategies to counter this game-changing technology.

## Gameplay Mechanics

### Resource System

- **Traditional Resources:**
  - Credits (universal currency)
  - Raw Materials (construction)
  - Power (facility operation)
- **Unique Resources:**
  - AFN: Quantum Processing Power
  - EC: Industrial Capacity
  - PI: Salvage Points

### Victory Conditions

1. **Territorial Control:** Secure and hold strategic quantum computing facilities
2. **Economic Dominance:** Control 70% of the map's resource nodes
3. **Technical Superiority:** Research and deploy all faction-specific superweapons

### Special Features

#### Adaptive Battlefield

- Dynamic weather systems affect unit performance
- Destructible environments can be weaponized
- Territory control affects resource generation and unit capabilities

#### Commander Abilities

Each faction has unique commander powers that unlock through gameplay:

- AFN: Global satellite strikes, cyber attacks
- EC: Mass production surges, EMP blackouts
- PI: Guerrilla operations, market manipulation

## Campaign Structure

### AFN Campaign: "Ghost Protocol"

Follow General Sarah Chen as she leads the AFN's effort to protect democracy while wrestling with the moral implications of AI-driven warfare.

### EC Campaign: "Iron Dynasty"

Join Commander Wei Zhang in his mission to unite the Eastern Collective and prove that human determination can triumph over technological superiority.

### PI Campaign: "Phoenix Rising"

Experience the rise of the mysterious "Director" as they transform scattered resistance groups into a formidable global force.

## Backstory Timeline

- **2032:** Global economic collapse
- **2033:** Formation of the Eastern Collective
- **2034:** Alliance of Free Nations established
- **2035:** Phoenix Initiative emerges
- **2036:** Silicon Valley breakthrough
- **2037:** Operation Blackout
- **2038:** Present day conflict

Each faction's story interweaves with the others, creating a complex narrative web where players' choices in one campaign affect the storylines of the others, encouraging multiple playthroughs to experience the full scope of the conflict.

## Universal Features

### Unit Characteristics

- Each unit has unique veterancy bonuses
- Units can be customized with faction-specific upgrades
- Secondary abilities unlock at veteran status
- Units can be captured and converted by certain special units

### Building Mechanics

- Most structures require power to function
- Buildings can be garrisoned by infantry
- Defensive structures can be upgraded with faction-specific enhancements
- Production buildings can queue multiple units

### Economy System

- Main resources gathered by dedicated units
- Secondary resources provided by map control
- Tech buildings unlock new units and abilities
- Superweapon buildings have significant power requirements
