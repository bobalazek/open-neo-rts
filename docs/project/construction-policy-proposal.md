# Normal construction: proposed first-playable contract

Status: the owner [approved the normal radius, clearance and payment/refund rules](./decisions/2026-11-02-normal-construction.md), 2026-11-02. These are accepted design rules awaiting implementation. Missing paid headquarters recovery and extractor cost/time values remain pending; those recipes are unavailable. Debug spawning keeps its already accepted radius exception and occupied-cell refusal.

## Accepted rules

- **Normal expansion:** for each new footprint cell, measure its shortest squared distance to an occupied footprint-cell center of a completed structure belonging to the same Force. Each new cell must be within six cells of some provider (squared distance ≤36); use three cells for a defense or wall (≤9). Incomplete sites, enemies and other Forces do not provide radius. These provisional distances are chosen for the current 2×2–4×4 buildings and 128-cell Training Grounds, not claimed to be balanced.
- **First base:** initialize the headquarters from the faction kit at the selected map spawn; this initialization has no prior-base requirement. If every provider is lost, a surviving builder may rebuild its faction headquarters at that assigned spawn, subject to ordinary terrain/occupancy and payment. Other remote structures do not receive a general first-structure exception. Current Command Center content has no construction cost or build time; recovery requires separately approved or explicitly provisional missing values, never free/instant defaults inferred from start-kit initialization.
- **Shared extractor (separately approved):** use the compact 2×2 footprint and fixed grid node anchor in the [decision](./decisions/2026-11-01-neutral-extractor.md), avoiding exact-center parity between 1×1 oil and 2×2 material markers. Builders may establish it on a remote eligible node, but the extractor never extends base radius. Normal terrain, bounds, margins and unrelated occupancy still apply. Territory modes remain a separate later policy.
- **Clearance:** ask only controllable friendly units occupying the footprint to move to reachable legal cells outside it, in stable-ID order using normal pathfinding. Wait at most 40 ticks (two game seconds), then refuse if blocked. Enemies or units without a control grant refuse placement; never commandeer them or delete units. Cancellation or refusal ends the pending clearance operation without overriding newer player orders. This proposal does not settle cooperative allied-Force construction.
- **Payment:** choosing/previewing a building is free. After terrain, radius and clearance succeed, creating the construction site pays its full cost atomically. A pending placement spends nothing. Insufficient funds or a same-tick competing spend creates no site; site destruction loses its investment.
- **Cancellation/sale:** canceling an unfinished site refunds 100% of its paid cost, following the economy document. A completed structure sells for the existing 50%. Reject duplicate or unauthorized requests and never pay both a cancellation and a death/sale refund. The owner approved this resolution of the conflicting cancel descriptions.

The extractor remains the [separate producer](./neutral-extractor-plan.md): credits-only build cost, power consumption, materials from material nodes and supplemental credits from oil. Provisional debug health/vision/power (800/four/−10) are approved; price, construction time, rates and depletion still need a balance contract. No new fuel/power-conversion economy, cargo dock or faction builder is introduced.

## Why this shape

A completed-building radius prevents unfinished-site chains from extending the base for free. The assigned-spawn headquarters exception permits recovery without turning a lone builder into unrestricted remote base placement. Extractor access can reach contested resource sites without making each cheap node outpost a base anchor. A finite clearance window fits the existing shared path budget and allows a truthful refusal if a unit cannot move. Atomic site creation avoids tying up money while a player previews or clears a site.

[OpenRA separates radius-providing actors from buildable-area adjacency](https://docs.openra.net/en/release/traits/#baseprovider), and its [buildable-area rule](https://docs.openra.net/en/release/traits/#requiresbuildablearea) explicitly measures permitted proximity. This supports defining providers and distance semantics rather than saying “a few cells”; its numbers are not copied. Current maps and buildings need a playtest before these proposed distances become a balance claim. Sources checked 2026-11-02.

## Implementation and evidence

Record the accepted decision, reconcile the conflicting design text and activate 1B-41…1B-43 only for the accepted scope. Before node code, specify the exact node-anchor-to-rotated-footprint origin mapping. Before committing a waited placement, recheck current terrain, providers, occupancy, grants and funds through the authoritative path. Test boundary/one-cell-outside distances, rotated footprints, other Forces, incomplete sites, lost/rebuilt headquarters, node exclusivity, unreachable/trapped/newly arrived units, timeout/cancel/newer orders, concurrent spending and duplicate refunds in one affected batch. Outcome changes require a simulation-version bump and updated goldens. Preview must reuse the authoritative checker and expose exact pending/refusal reasons.

This proposal does not approve the first-playable roster or structure-target behavior. Those remain separate decisions; passing debug CI cannot decide them.
