# 2026-11-02 — Local Force notices and explored-cell pings

Under the owner's implementation autonomy, the first offline feed keeps eight
recent notices for 20 simulation seconds. Repeated damage on the same own actor
coalesces for five seconds. These are provisional presentation defaults; pause
freezes their age and they never change the simulation or consume commands.

Only copied controlled-seat snapshots are inputs: own health decreases,
newly observed own units after the initial baseline, construction completion,
and permitted death events associated with an already observed own actor.
Disappearance alone is not a loss: it can reflect a grant change. There is no
hidden-attacker lookup, position inference or enemy notification feed.

Attack, defend, on-my-way, danger and help pings are local annotations in this
offline match, explicitly described as such. Alt-click or the camera-location
button places them only in explored in-bounds terrain. Space and clickable
notices pan to the permitted event location; neither issues a unit order.
Shared pings, the radial wheel and announcer audio follow their actual systems.
