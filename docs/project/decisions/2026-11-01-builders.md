# 2026-11-01 — Builders construct structures

**Context.** Two construction models were possible: the headquarters builds structures in the background and the player places them, or builder units construct them on site. Wonder races, where teammates pool their builders on one site, need builder units.

**Decision.** Every faction has a `builder` role, trained at its headquarters: the Alliance's Combat Engineer, the Collective's Field Technician, the Phoenix Initiative's Nanite Constructor. Builders construct on site (more builders build faster, with diminishing returns), repair, capture, and are the only units that work on wonders. Allied builders can help teammates.

**Consequences.** Builders become valuable targets, and protecting them is part of play. Structure definitions don't list what builds them; any builder of the faction can. Losing every builder and the headquarters ends a Force's ability to build.
